    Preferences on your device

    Two interface settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and aren't sent to us.

    KeyPurpose
    candidflow-themeRemembers whether you chose the light theme.
    candidflow-sidebar-collapsedRemembers whether you collapsed the sidebar.

    Third-party services

    Fonts, icons and some interface scripts load from Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs and unpkg. Candidflow doesn't set cookies for these services, but they receive your IP address when your browser requests the files. See the privacy notice.

    Why there's no cookie banner

    Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), cookies that are strictly necessary for a service you've asked to use don't need consent. Every cookie above falls into that category. If we ever add a cookie that isn't essential, we'll ask first.

    Managing cookies

    You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking sessionid or csrftoken will stop you from signing in or saving forms. Clearing local storage resets your theme and sidebar preferences.
