    What we hold about you

    Your name, email address and phone number; the roles you applied for and where each application stands; your CV and the skills found in it; your interview dates; and the feedback interviewers write after each round.

    Emails you'll get

    Your application moves stage

    For example from screening to an HR interview, or to a final decision. The email shows the role and the new stage.

    An interview is scheduled

    The email shows the role, the type of interview and when it is.

    That's all we send. No newsletters and no marketing. If you think an email reached you by mistake, tell us at {{ contacts.help }}.

    How your CV is screened

    Your CV is automatically compared against the skills the role needs, which gives it a match score. That score doesn't decide anything. Someone from the recruitment team reads your CV and records whether it passes screening, and you're emailed when that happens.

    Keyword matching can miss things, like a skill described in different words, so it's worth naming your skills plainly in your CV. If you think an outcome is wrong, email {{ contacts.privacy }} with the role you applied for and we'll look again.

    Who sees your application

    Only the recruitment team: recruiters, the people interviewing you, senior reviewers and hiring managers. Technical interviewers mainly work with applications in their own department. Every change to interview feedback is recorded, including who made it.

    If we've invited you to set up an account, it shows your own applications and nothing else — no other candidates, no interview notes, and no match scores.

    How long we keep it

    If you're not offered the role, we delete your details, CV and interview records 12 months after the final decision. If you're hired, the relevant details move to your employee record.

    What you can ask for

    • A copy of everything we hold about you, including interview feedback.
    • Corrections to anything that's wrong.
    • Withdrawing your application and deleting your data.
    • An explanation of a screening outcome, or for it to be looked at again.

    Email {{ contacts.privacy }} and we'll reply within one month. The full details are in our privacy notice.
