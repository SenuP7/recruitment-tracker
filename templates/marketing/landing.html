How it works

Follow a candidate from first application to final decision.

Every application moves through the same stages, and every stage leaves a record the next person can build on.

  1. 01

    Applied

    A candidate record is created once and linked to each role they apply for. Duplicates are prevented by email.

  2. 02

    CV screening

    The CV is scored against the role's required and nice-to-have skills. Strong matches pass screening automatically; weak ones are marked as failed.

  3. 03

    HR interview

    Schedule the round, assign an interviewer, and it appears in their own interview list the moment it's saved.

  4. 04

    Technical interview

    Technical interviewers work from a dashboard scoped to their own department and record a rating, recommendation, and evidence.

  5. 05

    Senior review

    Reviewers read the full feedback thread, reply where something needs discussing, and every edit is kept in the history.

  6. 06

    Decision

    The application is accepted or rejected, and the whole path — scores, rounds, feedback — stays on the record.

Features

What it actually does

No pitch and no pricing — just the tools this team uses every day.

CV screening & match scoring

Every CV is scored against the role's requirements the moment it's uploaded, then ranked and categorized automatically.

Interview scheduling

Schedule rounds, assign an interviewer, and spot anything past its date that still needs an outcome.

Threaded feedback

Ratings, recommendations and replies, with author-locked edits and a full history of every change.

Role-based dashboard

A live pipeline, upcoming interviews and recent feedback — scoped to what your role is allowed to see.

Search & export

Find any candidate or position instantly, and export whatever is currently filtered straight to CSV.

Notifications

Know when someone edits or removes feedback you wrote, without going looking for it.
