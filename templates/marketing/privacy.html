    1. Who we are

    Candidflow ("we", "us") is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. It applies to people who apply for a role through us (candidates) and to staff who are given an account to run recruitment (users).

    For anything about your personal data, contact our data protection contact at {{ contacts.privacy }}. We handle personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

    If you're a candidate, the candidate notice covers the same ground in a shorter, plain-language form.

    2. What we collect

    About candidates

    • Contact details: first and last name, email address and phone number.
    • Application details: the positions you applied for, the department, the date you applied and where each application is in our process.
    • Your CV: the PDF or Word file you send us, the text we extract from it, and the skills we found in it.
    • Assessment records: CV match scores, interview dates, types and outcomes, and the feedback interviewers write, including ratings (1 to 5), recommendations, comments and replies.
    • Email delivery records: which notification emails were sent to you, when, and whether delivery succeeded.

    About staff users

    • Account details: username, name, email address, role and department. Passwords are only ever stored as one-way hashes.
    • Activity records: feedback you write, a log of every edit or deletion of feedback (who made it, when, and what changed), and in-app notifications sent to you.

    When you apply online

    • What you type into the application form: your name, email address, optional phone number, an optional message, and your CV.
    • The IP address you submitted from. It's used only to stop the form being flooded with automated submissions, and it's erased the moment you confirm your application.
    • The date you confirmed you'd read the candidate notice and terms.

    An unconfirmed application isn't part of our recruitment records. It sits unused, is never shown to the hiring team, and is deleted along with the CV if you don't confirm it. See retention.

    Technical data

    • Essential cookies that keep you signed in and protect forms. See the cookie notice.
    • Standard request logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, time and page requested.

    We don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't sell personal data.

    4. Automated CV screening

    When a CV is uploaded against an application, Candidflow compares its text against the skills the position requires (80% of the score) and the skills that are nice to have (20%), including recognised alternative names for each skill. This produces a match score.

    The score does not decide anything on its own. A member of the recruitment team reads the CV and records whether it passes screening. The score is there to help them prioritise, and the system will not mark an application as passed or failed until a person does it.

    That means no decision about your application is based solely on automated processing, so the restrictions in Article 22 of UK GDPR don't apply. You are still told when your application changes stage, and you can:

    • ask us why an outcome was reached,
    • give us your point of view, for example skills the keyword match missed, and
    • ask for the decision to be looked at again.

    Email {{ contacts.privacy }} with the position you applied for.

    5. Who can see it and who processes it

    Inside Candidflow, access depends on a person's role. Recruiters, HR interviewers, senior reviewers and leadership managers work across all departments, and technical interviewers see the dashboard for their own department. Details are on the security page.

    We use these service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

    ProviderWhat for
    Amazon Web ServicesHosting the application and database, storing CVs (Amazon S3), sending emails (Amazon SES), queueing notifications (Amazon SQS) and logging email delivery (Amazon DynamoDB)
    Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs (Cloudflare), unpkgDelivering fonts, icons and interface scripts to your browser. These services receive your IP address when your browser requests the files.

    We only share personal data with anyone else when the law requires us to.

    6. International transfers

    Our data is stored in the AWS Asia Pacific (Singapore) region, which is outside the UK. These transfers are covered by AWS's data processing addendum, which includes the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses approved for UK transfers. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

    If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will unless we need to keep it for a legal claim.

    8. Your rights

    You have the right to:

    • Access: a copy of your data, including interview feedback written about you.
    • Correction: fix anything inaccurate or incomplete.
    • Erasure: have your data deleted, including withdrawing an application.
    • Restriction of how we use your data while a concern is looked into.
    • Objection to processing we base on legitimate interests.
    • Portability: receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format.
    • Human review of automated screening decisions (see section 4).

    Email {{ contacts.privacy }}. We'll respond within one month, free of charge, and may need to confirm your identity first.

    If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

    9. Keeping it secure

    Access is limited by role and checked on the server, passwords are hashed, CVs are stored privately and opened through links that expire after an hour, and every change to interview feedback is logged. The security page describes exactly what's in place.

    10. Changes to this notice

    We'll update this notice when how we handle data changes, and update the date at the top of the page. For significant changes we'll tell active candidates and users by email.
