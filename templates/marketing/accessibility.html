    Compliance status

    Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Candidflow is partially compliant: we've built for it and checked it ourselves, but it hasn't been independently audited, and there are known gaps listed below.

    What works well

    • Keyboard: every link, button and form can be reached with the keyboard, with a visible focus ring. Press / to jump to search.
    • Skip link: public pages have a "Skip to content" link as the first thing you tab to.
    • Motion: animations, count-ups and scroll effects turn off when your device asks for reduced motion.
    • Colour: application status is always shown with text and an icon, never colour alone.
    • Themes: dark and light themes are both available.
    • Zoom: layouts reflow down to phone width, so zooming in doesn't force sideways scrolling on text pages.
    • Screen readers: decorative backgrounds and illustrations are hidden from screen readers, and pages use a meaningful heading structure.

    Known limitations

    • The signed-in app doesn't yet have a skip link, so keyboard users tab through the sidebar on each page.
    • Pipeline charts show counts as text, but their bars have no separate text alternative.
    • Wide data tables scroll sideways on small screens.
    • Uploaded CVs are the candidate's own documents, and we can't guarantee they're accessible.
    • When the dashboard filters update results, the change isn't announced to screen readers.

    We plan to fix the first and last of these as the app's pages are reworked.

    Reporting a problem

    If something blocks you, or you need information in a different format, email {{ contacts.help }} with the page and what happened. We'll reply within 5 working days.

    Enforcement

    The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) enforces the Equality Act 2010 in Great Britain. If you're not happy with our response, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service.
