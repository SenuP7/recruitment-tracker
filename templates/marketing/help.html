For staff

Using Candidflow

How do I get an account?

Staff accounts are created by an administrator. Ask your manager to request one through {{ contacts.help }}, stating the role and department you need. Candidates don't need one of these: applying online creates a candidate account for you.

I forgot my password.

Use the "Forgot your password?" link on either sign-in page. We'll email you a link to set a new one. If the email doesn't arrive, contact {{ contacts.help }}.

Why can't I see the dashboard or CV screening?

Both are limited to recruitment staff: recruiters, HR and technical interviewers, senior reviewers and leadership managers. Technical interviewers also need a department on their profile, otherwise the dashboard stays empty. If your role looks wrong, contact help.

I got a "403 Forbidden" page.

Your role doesn't include that action. For example, only some roles can create positions or delete candidates. If you need it for your job, ask for a role change rather than using someone else's account.

Who can edit feedback I wrote?

You, senior reviewers and leadership managers. If someone else edits or deletes your feedback, you'll get a notification, and every change is kept in the feedback's history.

How is the CV match score worked out?

The CV's text is checked for each skill the position lists, including known alternative names (for example "JS" for JavaScript). Required skills make up 80% of the score and nice-to-have skills 20%. The score never sets the outcome: a recruiter reads the CV and confirms whether it passed screening. Keyword matching can miss experience described in other words, so treat a low score as a prompt to look, not a verdict.

Which CV files can I upload?

PDF or Word (.docx), up to 5 MB. Scanned image-only PDFs have no text to read, so they'll score low.

How do I export applications?

On the dashboard, set the filters you want and choose Export CSV. The file contains exactly what those filters show, limited to what your role can see. It contains personal data, so follow the handling rules.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Press / anywhere in the app to jump to search. The theme switch in the top bar toggles light and dark, and your choice is remembered on that device.

For applicants

Your application

How do I apply, and what happens next?

Pick a role on our open roles page and send the short form with your CV. We email you a link to confirm the address is yours; until you click it, nothing reaches the hiring team. After that you can set a password and follow your application, and we'll email you when it changes stage or an interview is booked. We never send marketing. See the candidate notice.

My CV didn't pass screening. Can a person look at it?

Yes. Email {{ contacts.privacy }} with the role you applied for, and a recruiter will review your CV and tell you the result.

How do I see or delete my data?

Email {{ contacts.privacy }}. You can ask for a copy of everything we hold, including interview feedback, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your application and have it deleted. We reply within one month.
